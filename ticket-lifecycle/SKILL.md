# Ticket lifecycle

A ticket is a handoff to someone who has not seen your session and does not have your machine.

- **Self-contained:** a teammate can understand the problem, judge the evidence, and start work from the ticket and shared systems alone.
- **Portable:** nothing in it works only for you. Evidence that lives locally is moved into the ticket or somewhere shared.
- **Durable pointers:** context the ticket relies on is named by references that still resolve later and that a reader can follow without asking you, such as permalinks, IDs, and commits, not "the thread from Tuesday" or a link that moves.
- **Honest about time:** a ticket records a moment, so evidence carries its date and source.
- **True for its whole life:** as work moves, the ticket keeps saying where things stand, and it closes with the outcome.
